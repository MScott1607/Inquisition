Our Friends

Led by a member of Clan Lavellan, the Inquisition has worked with the Dalish to purge Venatori threats across the Dales and has several Dalish recruits.
We are collaborating with the prosperous cities in the Free Marches to facilitate trade and quell the threats that visit our world.
The Breach threatens all, and with Corypheus unleashing further darkspawn upon the world, The great city of Orzammar has declared support, lending us several warriors from the famed Legion of the Dead. Fearless, powerful darkspawn hunters.
The Empress of Orlais recognises the threat to our world, and has pledged military as well as financial aid.
The Grey Wardens have pledged their finest to our cause. They have already eliminated local darkspawn hoardes, protecting countless civilians.
The mage rebellion has been quelled and the remaining mages have pledged service to the Inquisition. Their power has already been used to stabilise the Breach at Haven, and heal many wounded civilians and soldiers.
