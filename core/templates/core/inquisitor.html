The Herald, The Inquisitor

An introduction to our Herald; the man who will lead us from destruction, who will heal the tear in the Veil and deliver us from the evil that created it.

Inquisitor Lavellan

The Herald is a member of Clan Lavellan, a roaming Dalish clan from the Free Marches and a gifted warrior. His unique background has given him keen survival skills, and his attendance at The Conclave demonstrates his natural curiosity and desire for knowledge and cooperation between all inhabitants of Thedas that makes him a uniquely qualified leader.

As the sole witness to events of the Conclave, and sole survivor of the explosion that destroyed The Temple of Sacred Ashes, The Herald also emerged from the Fade with the unique ability to heal tears in the Veil.

How did he get here?

The Herald attended the Conclave as a representative of the Dalish clans, knowing the outcome would affect everything and everyone in Thedas.

During the Conclave, a force of evil beyond our reckoning annihilated the Temple of Sacred Ashes. We do not yet know his reasons, but we do know that everyone except The Herald was killed - including Divine Justinia V, who was sacrificed to a blood magic ritual to open The Fade.

Having witnessed the brutal sacrifice of Divine Justinia V, The Herald was thrust into The Fade by powerful magic. On awakening, he was threatened by giant spider-like creatures but survived when he was guided out to safety by a female spirit.

He collapsed on emergence from The Fade, but awoke with a new power - a power to heal tears in the Veil.
