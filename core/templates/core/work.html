What We Do

We are primarily focused on closing The Breach, but our investigations into this has led us to uncovering more systemic problems throughout Ferelden, The Free Marches, and even Orlais. Here, you can read about some of the work we have done to improve our communities and help those in need. You will see that not all of it is heavy work!

The Herald himself attended to refugees in the Hinterlands near Redcliffe, using his skills as a Dalish hunter to provide fresh food for those in need.
We have built watch towers in the Hinterlands, allowing for advance warnings to locals and Inquisition patrols of incoming attacks.
Vanquished Demon-possessed wolves from the Redcliffe farmlands, allowing locals to continue making a living.
Reunited a lost druffalo - Druffy - with her owner (yes, really!)
Eliminated outlaws on the East Road, reported to be confronting refugees and killing any who dared to defend themselves.
Collected herbal ingredients for local healers who were unable to travel due to the dangers present in the Hinterlands, allowing potions and salves to be distributed to refugees in need.
Worked with the Imperial Army to eliminate demons plaguing travellers and merchants along the road between Val Foret and Montsimmard.
Stopped the late Marquise Bouffon's mining activity on behalf of the Venatori, and working with The Grey Wardens to eliminate the Darkspawn hoarde she unleashed.
Wiped out a Red Templar holdout on the Storm Coast, restoring an old Dwarven port and facilitating trade and supply routes between Ferelden and Orlais.
