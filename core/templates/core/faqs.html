To put it concisely, we were somewhat of a back-up plan by Divine Justinia V in case of a calamity within the Chantry, or in case her attempts to broker a mage-templar peace failed. We were officially formed before the disaster at the Conclave, but through these events we gained prominence due to the appearance of The Herald. Now, we focus on maintaining the peace, closing rifts, aiding civilians and refugees and - of course - defeating Corypheus and the Venatori. If you would like a more in-depth look at our history and purpose, please visit our About page.

The Elder One is a Darkspawn, claiming to be an ancient Tevinter Magister and calls himself 'Corypheus'. You may see him referred to as both names here. He claims to have been succesful in entering the Fade and plans to restore Tevinter to - what he describes as - "its former glory". This is why he has allied so closely with the Venatori.

Similar to an Archdemon, he is able to control Darkspawn through the Taint, and the Taint is spread rapidly via Red Lyrium - lyrium that has been corrupted with the Taint.

We have several prominent Chantry clerics within our ranks, as well as the closest aides to Divine Justinia leading us. Despite the Inquisition having been begun again by Divine Justinia herself, many within the Chantry have chosen to denounce the Inquisition and the Herald. They call us heretics. It is currently not our position to be concerned with this; whilst we would of course ideally have the full backing of the Chantry, this is difficult without a Divine in place. We feel it is more important to focus on the immediate threat to our world, rather than in-fighting between clergy.

The Venatori are an extremist cult of Tevinter supremacists. They sow chaos throughout Thedas for their master, The Elder One, as they believe he will restore Tevinter to glory. They claim to be protectors of Tevinter, despite not being officially sanctioned by anyone in Tevinter. However, some Magisters are part of the Venatori cult. Such is their desire for Tevinter's supreme rule, they have deceived the Grey Wardens into in-fighting, plotted an assassination of Empress Celene, attacked refugees throughout the Dales, and allied with the Red Templars.

Typically found in crystalline form, just as normal lyrium. Whilst the familiar blue lyrium is mined by Dwarves, Red Lyrium has been able to spread across Thedas - both above and below ground. It has never been encountered by the Mining caste before, so we can assume it is linked with Corypheus himself.

It thins the Veil, which allows demons to penetrate the physical realm; it is for this reason that there have been so many demon attacks recently. Unlike normal lyrium, it does not need mining or refining before ingestion. It has been discovered that Red Lyrium is normal lyrium, tainted by the Blight.

It is extremely dangerous; please do not touch it, and avoid going too near. If you are able, please report the location to your nearest Inquisition outpost.

Red Templars are former Templars who have succumbed to Corypheus' manipulations. Some were floundering Templars, rallied by a former Templar named Samson during the mage-templar war. Others were following orders and their duty, but unknowingly pulled into this new life under Seeker Lucius - a man who had abandoned his duties in service to Corypheus, and gave himself up to demonic possession.

Both factions use ingested Red Lyrium to enhance their abilities, although they were not told of the monstrous side effects this would have on them. Some Templars appear to be more affected than others, although the deforming of their bodies appears consistent. Few retain their humanity, and we do not fully understand why. What we do assume to be true, is that through the taint in the red lyrium, Corypheus is able to control them more and more the further they are corrupted with its use.

Despite being the son of a Magister, Dorian Pavus is estranged from his family and ostracised by most of Tevinter for opposing almost everything they consider their greatest strengths and sources of pride.

In fact, the initial meeting with Dorian Pavus involved him saving the Herald's life - at great risk to his own. We understand there will be a lack of trust in him amongst our followers and recruits, but we ask that people trust our judgement and do not cause any trouble for The Herald for the decision to keep him in our service. We assure you, all background checks have been carried out and verified multiple times by multiple Inquisition staff, and he is being monitored closely.

The Iron Bull has been completely open with The Herald since the day they met, and The Chargers are a renowned mercenary company. The Qunari are practical above all else, and recognise the threat that Corypheus and The Breach hold to everyone. The Iron Bull is no different.
